DGP's Message

  • Odisha Police is committed to deliver good governance in the state through use of innovations in the field of communication and information technology and in this process it has taken a major step by introducing an ambitious as well as mission mode project namely “Crime & Criminal Tracking Network and System (CCTNS)”.
  • Odisha Police is committed to delivering time-bound services to citizens by the use of the new-age technology. The purpose of the Citizen Portal is to provide a number of services to citizens on the internet. Currently we are providing 18 services through this portal. We will be adding more services to this bouquet in the future. We are constantly looking for ways through which extra services can be availed by residents without physically visiting a Police Station or other offices.
  • People may download our Mobile App “Sahayata”, available on Android and iOS platforms, for availing these services using mobile phone devices.

We look forward to your feedback to improving experience of citizens in getting quality service from the police promptly.

About Citizen Portal

Odisha Police is implementing Crime and Criminal Tracking Network and Systems (CCTNS) which is a Mission Mode Project of Government of India. Under CCTNS Project all Police Stations and Higher Police Offices in the State and across the Country is being computerized and networked. For convenience all Police offices are interconnected and work is undertaken in a centralized application.

Odisha Police is committed towards time bound delivery of services to the Citizens and provide good governance in the State. In order to provide better and quick services to Citizens, Odisha Police has launched Online "Citizen Portal" under CCTNS Project. "Citizen Portal" will enable citizens to access information, request for different Police services, complaints and to get copy of FIR as well as to track the status of different services provided to the Citizens. To avail the services create a user account provided in the right side panel of this page and log into this Portal. To know more click on "Help".

About Event/ Performance

Request for Event/Performance is among one of the citizen services provided by Odisha Police under CCTNS Project.

This service is available to the citizen who are residing in the area of Commissionerate of police Cuttack and Bhubaneswar and to the citizens residing other places of the state where (30 Police Act) has been promulgated by the district authority.

  • To avail the service, Citizen has to log in to the Portal.
  • After logging into the system, please update your profile immediately and upload your photo, Identity Proof, Address Proof etc. for all services and for future references
  • While filling the request form, please fill at least all the mandatory fields (*) along with Personal details, address, contact number and event details.
  • Before submitting the request, Please verify the same. Once you submit, the request will not be editable.
  • After submission of the request you will be alerted by the system by SMS and email along with Service Request No.
  • Please get a system generated print copy after successfully submitting the request for future reference.

If you are an existing user to Citizen Portal, click here to 'Login' otherwise click 'Register' if you are a new User.
